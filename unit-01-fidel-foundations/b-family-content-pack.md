## 3. Lesson build: one family, seven small steps

Every form follows the same calm loop:

1. **Meet it** — the large Fidel form appears first.
2. **Hear it** — one slow native recording, then one natural-speed recording.
3. **See the family** — the active form lights up in `በ ቡ ቢ ባ ቤ ብ ቦ`.
4. **Trace it** — follow an approved stroke animation with a finger.
5. **Connect it** — show a verified picture-word anchor, or a form-pattern activity when no natural word is available.
6. **Build it** — select the matching form from the seven-family strip.
7. **Return later** — one gentle “sound detective” review after other teaching.
