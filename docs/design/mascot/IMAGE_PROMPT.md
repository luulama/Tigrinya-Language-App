# Mascot concept exploration

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, not CLI/API fallback. The generated three-concept board is saved alongside this file as `concept-exploration.png`. Production assets were then refined into local editable SVG code so the same character and mouth geometry can be reused across states and still steps.

The notebook direction was selected. Generated face studies are expressive concept art, not phonetic evidence.

## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original mascot concept sheet for a calm Tigrinya Fidäl learning app.
Primary request: Explore THREE distinct original character directions on one landscape art-director concept board, with three equally sized full-body front/three-quarter figures and one face close-up for each. LEFT: a terracotta hyrax pathfinder with rounded short ears, cream muzzle, tiny teal satchel and page-shaped ear notch. CENTER: an anthropomorphic folded notebook explorer with an asymmetrical folded paper corner, cream page face, warm copper binding, teal bookmark tail and small walking boots; use a clearly readable expressive human-like mouth and two different curious eyebrows. RIGHT: a warm brown-skinned small human pathfinder with a simple cropped curled hair silhouette, teal overshirt, terracotta trousers and a small two-tab notebook; no cultural costume. Each option must have a memorable precise silhouette, kind expressive eyes, a mouth that remains readable in a 64-pixel icon, and a purposeful friendly personality.
Style: polished flat vector-friendly editorial character design with softly textured paper surfaces, clear intentional shapes, elegant restrained outlines, warm children’s book craft, comfortable for adults too. Color palette: deep ink #273849, cream #fff8e9, teal #176f58, terracotta #c86443 and small gold #e5b66d. White background, no scene or app UI.
Constraints: These are exploratory drawings, NOT phonetic diagrams. No sound labels, no Tigrinya text, no claimed authentic clothing or folklore. No logos, lettering, captions, watermarks or brand reference. Avoid owls, competitor silhouettes, emoji, generic blobs, beaks obscuring mouth, huge baby eyes, teeth grins, noisy accessories. Include neutral, attentive and gentle encouragement face studies. Original designs only.
