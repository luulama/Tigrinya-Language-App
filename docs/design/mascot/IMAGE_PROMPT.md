## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: professional original bear mascot reference sheet for a calm Tigrinya literacy app, to be implemented as lightweight SVG.
Primary request: Design ONE cohesive living cinnamon-brown bear named Neri across six expressive poses on a clean cream background: welcoming, listening with a head tilt, thoughtful, gently encouraging, joyful celebration, and curled up sleepy. No written labels or text. Include a larger full-body seated welcoming pose and five smaller consistent animal poses. The bear has natural round forepaws, broad hind paws, a short tail, soft rounded ears, an oval cream muzzle, a dark rounded triangular nose, warm expressive eyebrows and a tiny three-point crown tuft. A slightly lighter tuft at one ear gives an identifiable asymmetry. Give the animal appealing but restrained proportions, readable amber-dark eyes, a grounded body and a calm friendly personality. The silhouette should read as a BEAR at 48px. Seated and four-pawed natural animal poses, NOT a humanoid figure. Do not give it human hands, clothing, shoes, books, stationery, backpacks or objects. Do not make it a human in a bear suit. It must feel charming to children and adults, not babyish or generic clip art.
Style: polished original editorial character illustration, vector-friendly curving silhouettes with a little dimensional shading and very restrained paper texture. Intentional contours, beautiful simple masses, consistent facial structure across every pose. Palette: cinnamon #b77349, darker warm brown #734b35, cream #f2d4a5, dark ink #273849; the app uses teal #176f58 around the character but the bear wears no accessories.
Pronunciation relevance: give the muzzle an unobscured mouth area; these are expressive character studies, NOT phonetic diagrams or a claim about a real sound. No human lips attached to the bear.
Avoid: book or object bodies, humanoid standing posture, giant baby eyes, emoji, stock-clip-art look, mascot resemblance to competitors, scary claws/teeth, folklore costumes, text, logos, watermarks.
