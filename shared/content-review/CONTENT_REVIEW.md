# Tigrinya Content Review Workflow

Every learner-facing item must be approved by a qualified native Tigrinya reviewer before release.

## Review record

Fidäl writing records now live in `fidal-writing.json`; all 293 current character stroke/audio records remain pending. See [Fidäl writing requirements](../../FIDAL_WRITING.md) for source locators, reviewed directions, handwriting style, rights and normal/slow native audio. Existing Amharic material is not automatically Tigrinya approval. Printed font outlines and learner-drawn strokes are not teacher stroke-order evidence.

For each word, sentence, audio clip, and exercise, record:

- Tigrinya script
- English meaning
- Transliteration
- Intended register and context
- Dialect / variation notes, if applicable
- Audio speaker and recording date
- Reviewer name, decision, and revision notes

## Required checks

1. Translation and spelling are correct.
2. The phrase is natural in modern everyday Tigrinya.
3. The transliteration matches the audio and agreed app standard.
4. The example is suitable for a beginner and its answer choices are unambiguous.
5. Pronunciation audio is clear, native, and licensed for the app.
6. Cultural, regional, and register variations are labelled instead of silently mixed.
