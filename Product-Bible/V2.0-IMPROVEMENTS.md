# Version 2.0 — Improvements Only

## Product architecture improvements

| ID | Affected sections | Contradiction, missing system, or weak assumption | Required replacement or addition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RV01 | 02/03/14 | Support labels are discussed but a single observation could still be interpreted differently across systems. | Use shared support categories: access accommodation, interaction guidance, meaning/model help, answer-revealing help, feedback retry, unknown support. Preserve unknowns and skill-specific interpretation. |
| RV02 | 04/05/14 | Stage coverage could be reduced to a percentage that hides missing critical criteria. | Define critical versus supporting criteria, permissible alternatives, and unassessed states. No aggregate can compensate for a missing critical criterion. |
| RV03 | 05 | Programme purposes lack a concrete whole-course authoring map. | Add named planning units across all eight stages, with module contracts, checkpoint obligations, and explicit distinction from authored Tigrinya lessons or the earlier 224 assignments. |
| RV04 | 06 | Sound-linked prerequisites could unintentionally block reading access for a learner using another valid route. | Model reviewed alternate evidence routes and access conditions explicitly. Do not silently award auditory evidence or remove reading access because hearing/speech evidence is absent. |
| RV05 | 07/18 | “Essential” in a broad catalogue could be interpreted as every mechanism being mandatory at launch. | Define one release-scoped selection with explicit exclusions; mechanism cost excludes authoring/review/rights and is not an implementation promise. |
| RV06 | 08 | A game can be completed through guessing even when its objective is named correctly. | Review cue leakage, chance completion, and response consequences; compare against an equally taught non-game task, not mere engagement or no activity. |

## Learning-science improvements

| ID | Affected sections | Educational or measurement risk | Required replacement or addition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RV07 | 09 | A picture or narrative memory may supply a purported reading answer. | Keep story interpretation, audio-supported understanding, and print decoding distinct; use appropriate cue-controlled checks with contextual support recorded. |
| RV08 | 10 | Human rubric judgment is treated as valid without a correction/appeal path. | Calibrate raters, record criterion disagreement and support, permit reconsideration, and prevent one opaque score from defining a learner. |
| RV09 | 11 | Replay may be inconsistently classified as assistance across listening tasks. | Specify replay allowance per task before use; distinguish permitted repeat listening from answer-revealing model exposure and preserve transcript effects. |
| RV10 | 12 | Generic Unicode testing does not prove that answer comparison preserves relevant Tigrinya distinctions. | Require qualified equivalent/non-equivalent test pairs plus original input preservation; report unsupported input separately from language error. |
| RV11 | 13 | A later cultural correction could make a learner appear wrong retroactively. | Withdraw or amend faulty tasks, preserve historical context, and provide an appropriate correction without blaming the learner. |
| RV12 | 14 | “Independent” may imply access to all external assistance, which the app cannot observe. | Describe observed conditions and unknown assistance explicitly. Do not claim device events prove no human/off-device help. |
| RV13 | 14 | An available FSRS adapter could be mistaken for a server-active or calibrated memory system. | Pin authoritative algorithm/version, separate interval scheduling from M3 criteria, and require a state migration and held-out evaluation before switching. |
| RV14 | 02/04/18 | Usability thresholds, delayed-learning pilots, and causal evaluation can collapse into one gate. | Use separate evidence/claims: usability, scoped learning change, attribution, and operational readiness. Predefine analysis and include missing follow-up and excluded contexts. |
