## Company purpose and first promise

Help learners build meaningful Tigrinya literacy and communication through accurate, accessible, culturally grounded learning, beginning with children developing early reading and writing. First deliver a complete reviewed foundation sequence for the primary 4–8 learner situation, especially where familiar spoken meaning needs a bridge to Fidäl. Broader audiences and advanced/professional capabilities are explicitly designed future directions, not launch promises.

## The educational system

Meaningful language knowledge, attention/access, memory, purposeful practice, and learner agency interact. The teaching loop establishes meaning, focuses a relevant relation, models and guides, corrects, revisits independently, offers appropriate changed-context use, and selects a useful next step. A learner can enter through existing knowledge and can request help or stop.

Maintain separate evidence for reading, writing, listening, speech, interaction, and context. Exposure is not memory; a corrected retry is not independent performance; a timed click is not fluency. Unknown assistance remains unknown. Proposed M0–M3 and scheduling thresholds are product policies requiring evaluation, not biological laws.

## The product system

The eight-stage framework and language-specific skill graph organise objectives and prerequisites without age ranks. The curriculum supplies reviewed meanings, models, tasks, later evidence, and connected use. Activities and games are reusable mechanisms, not new curricula for every picture. Story, speaking, listening, writing, and culture systems share the same objective, support, version, and evidence contracts.

The exact two-Ethiopic-grapheme constraint remains for Unit 2 candidates. Tigrinya is an abugida in Ge’ez/Ethiopic script, written left to right. No pronunciation, translation, grammar example, cultural association, or stroke order is invented to make a feature complete. Other languages require their own script, content, review, and evaluation adapters; infrastructure alone does not provide universal pedagogy.

## The trust and content system

Publication requires qualified independent approval for exact content/media versions and all relevant scopes. Keep source provenance, sense distinctions, rights, and correction history. Public delivery uses published snapshots; private learner evidence and unpublished review media have separate access boundaries. Offline public practice cannot pretend protected answer keys remain secret, and permanently disconnected devices cannot guarantee immediate revocation.

A suitable release has native models, understandable directions, fair tasks, access alternatives, technical recovery, correction capacity, and truthful claims. Missing content is a release gap, not an invitation to publish AI-generated certainty.

## Strategy and operating system

The four-person planning team needs additional qualified contributor/reviewer capacity where required; a role title cannot supply absent expertise. Budget a complete objective, including media, rights, review, QA, and correction, rather than only developer time. Keep the free foundation meaningful and do not sell relief from mistakes or essential help.

MVP, V1, V2, V3, and V4 represent expanding capability under gates, not calendar guarantees. Year 1/2/3/5/10 horizons remain conditional. AI is optional, draft assistance is not approval, and learner-facing AI requires separately demonstrated suitability. The existing modular-monolith code is preserved and inspected for gaps after documentation, not rewritten incidentally.
