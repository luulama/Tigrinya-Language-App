**Checked:** 20 November 2026. **Status:** Supporting register; not a claim of independent expert approval.

## Evidence sources and use

[Section 02](02-How-Humans-Learn.md) contains fourteen linked research references and their access basis: language fluency, pronunciation dimensions, interaction, automatization, L2 spacing, classroom memory strategies, retrieval versus elaboration, multilingual reading, abugida literacy, oral corrective feedback, motivation, lexical consolidation, cognitive science in classrooms, and early literacy relationships. Preserve its boundary statements; research from one language or age group is not automatic validation of Tigrinya instruction.

The remaining sections use that synthesis for general mechanisms and label their product contracts as design decisions. No feature effect-size ranking, universal age cutoff, vocabulary count, completion-hour norm, or biological mastery threshold is established here.

## Decision classes

**Approved commitment:** FINAL_V1 vision only. **Research finding:** a bounded source-supported statement. **Design policy:** a proposed operational contract such as EVIDENCE_V1, stage labels, workflow or interface convention. **Estimate:** a workload/cost hypothesis. **Content dependency:** an item requiring qualified sources, rights, models, or review. **Validation gap:** a learning, usability, access, technical, operational, commercial, or jurisdiction-specific question still requiring appropriate evidence.

Keep those classes separate in reviews and marketing. Proposed study-hour envelopes are planning quantities, not researched Tigrinya acquisition rates. M3 and the 24-hour/7-day conventions require pilot evaluation. Cultural context may support meaningful learning; the Bible does not prove an independent cultural-retention effect.

## Research maintenance

For a consequential change, record source title/link, publication date, access basis, population/task/comparator, relevant finding, uncertainty, affected section and decision. Read primary research or official standards rather than copying search snippets or competitor claims. Check new technical recommendations against current maintainer documentation and the actual repository. Do not rewrite locked commitments silently when evidence changes.

The breadth and quality of evidence should match the claim. A formative usability observation cannot establish causal effectiveness, an adult-language study cannot set a preschool mastery threshold, and a correct algorithm cannot establish pedagogical validity.
