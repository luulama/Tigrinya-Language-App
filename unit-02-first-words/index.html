    Pictures lead the way

    Look introduces the picture, draft word and existing English concept. Make it keeps a model visible while fitting two Fidäl character forms. Find it offers only introduced written words. The lion and hand provide repeatable visual demonstrations. Short prompts and large pictures guide each task.

    Activities normally use three pictures, followed by pair play, a recap and a cumulative picture-word check. A chosen picture also has a complete short activity. Wrong answers reveal a model and allow retry; supported items return once later. Results distinguish matching without help from practice with help. One-option checks are guided. Next words mixes familiar and new candidates; Review revisits practised words. Keep pictures is optional. In-memory counts are not reading, pronunciation, retention or approval. About 3–5 minutes is a planning estimate, not a timer.

    What independent learning still needs

    Navigation and picture/shape play work without reading a paragraph or asking a grown-up to explain a button. A child cannot learn reliable Tigrinya pronunciation from this silent preview. Approved native word recordings and short spoken directions are required before releasing independent language lessons.

    There are no child accounts, ads, purchases, scores, streaks or countdowns. Completed checks and preferences can be saved on this device only when you choose Save practice on this device. My practice & settings lets you stop saving or clear this app’s history. The shelf, drawings and unfinished activities remain in memory only. The settings entry is a two-tap separation, not a security or authentication gate.
