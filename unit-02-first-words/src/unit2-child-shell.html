    Pictures lead the way

    The child chooses a picture, sees its draft two-letter word, matches its picture, and fits the two letter shapes. Model pictures and letters stay visible. A small lion and animated hand provide repeatable visual demonstrations. No child-facing instruction is longer than a few words.

    Play offers three pictures at a time. A chosen picture can also be played on its own. A mismatch offers a visual cue without a penalty. Only explicit Keep saves pictures in this tab; this does not record mastery or approval.

    What independent learning still needs

    Navigation and picture/shape play work without reading a paragraph or asking a grown-up to explain a button. A child cannot learn reliable Tigrinya pronunciation from this silent preview. Approved native word recordings and short spoken directions are required before releasing independent language lessons.

    There are no child accounts, ads, purchases, scores, streaks, countdowns or persistent learner data. The shelf and unfinished play session are held in memory only. The settings entry is a two-tap separation, not a security or authentication gate.
