# Unit 2 — First three picture words

**Reviewer-ready draft, not approved learner content.** Every spelling, transliteration, meaning, image association, prompt and future recording awaits qualified native Tigrinya review.

Audience: ages 4–8. Duration target: 3–5 minutes, not yet measured with children. Classification: MVP learning-flow refinement using the existing vocabulary and offline architecture, not a new content release.

## 1. Learning goal

Notice a pictured concept and its printed two-letter word, fit the two letter shapes using a model, then recognize the word among forms already introduced. The first recognition step is a guided example. None of these interactions proves decoding ability, pronunciation accuracy or mastery.

## 3. Teacher script

These English notes are for adult review, not paragraphs for the child interface.

1. **Introduce — Look:** Point to the picture, then its complete printed word. Do not guess pronunciation from a transliteration.
2. **Demonstrate:** Point out the two displayed shapes; follow the lion's hand demonstration.
3. **Reinforce — Make it:** Place both shapes with the model visible. Compare the completed arrangement with the picture and whole-word model before continuing.
4. **Review and recognize — Find it:** The first example offers one printed word with its model visible. Later pictures offer two, then three introduced words. Reveal the model with the lion whenever needed.
5. **Repeat:** Introduce each new picture before its word can become a distractor. Allow stopping and repetition without penalties.

Qualified native reviewers must write and approve future spoken directions and natural word recordings. No Tigrinya directions or pronunciations are invented here.

## 4. Child activities

- Look at one picture, its whole word and two letter shapes.
- Tap the shapes into two places using the visible model.
- Choose the printed word for the picture, rather than copying an identical image. The first choice is guided; later options use only introduced forms.
- Revisit the same pictures and printed forms in the existing six-card pair game. Its design target is under three minutes without a countdown, not a validated completion time or reading assessment.
- Explicit Keep saves pictures in this tab only. Leaving and resuming remain possible.

## 5. Reinforcement

The same picture and whole word recur during introduction, supported assembly, post-answer review and pair play. The lion's model can be requested repeatedly. Feedback is brief and never claims mastery. Do not replace this repetition with points, streaks or extra mini-games.

## 6. Review section

Compare the completed letter arrangement with the whole-word model before recognition. The first recognition example keeps that model visible. A mismatch or requested hint reveals it again without recording a score or granting approval.

Same-picture alternative headwords are not competing recognition answers; duplicate artwork becomes one pair in the memory board. After native approval and usable recordings, observe independent navigation and recovery, then assess actual word/meaning recognition and pronunciation separately with qualified educators. The prototype does not collect those observations automatically.

## 8. Reviewer notes

Check spelling, transliteration, natural modern usage, pronunciation, age suitability, regional variation, context, both letter shapes, exact picture association and answer ambiguity. A shared house image does not establish that the wider bank's two house headwords are interchangeable.

No reviewer identity, decision or approval date is invented. Status remains **awaiting qualified native Tigrinya review**. AI pre-review and code checks cannot replace qualified review.

Risks: silent matching can be solved without understanding spoken Tigrinya; a guided example does not prove independent recognition; timing, real keyboard focus, responsive layout, assistive-technology behavior and child usability need direct testing. Keep prototype warnings visible.
